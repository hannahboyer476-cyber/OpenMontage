# The Emerald Line — Style & Concept Brief

Book: *The Emerald Line: Chronicles of the Sovereign Awakening*
Reference image: ornate emerald-green tome on a mossy rock in a sunlit forest.
Target pipeline: `cinematic` (book trailer). Runtime (Remotion vs. HyperFrames) is pending user approval.

## Style Brief

| Aspect | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| Palette | Deep emerald, antique silver, muted gold, moss green, warm amber light shafts |
| Motifs | Celtic knotwork borders, faceted emerald gems, central sigil, green ribbon bookmark |
| Lighting | Soft god-rays through forest canopy, dappled highlights, hazy depth |
| Camera | Shallow depth of field, slow dolly/push-in, macro details on the cover |
| Mood | Mythic, quiet, reverent; fantasy-tome gravitas |
| Sound | Low cello/drone, sparse harp or chime accents, forest ambience |
| Typography | Engraved serif, silver/gold on dark green; knotwork-style dividers |

## Concepts

1. **The Forest Remembers** — Continuous slow dolly around the book; light moves from dawn to dusk while the sigil glows and the title builds. Needs motion clips or a `threejs_world` scene. Leans Remotion (video-led).
2. **Knotwork Awakens** — Cover knotwork animates and grows vines into the forest, ending on a title card. Kinetic and typographic; lowest generation cost. Leans HyperFrames.
3. **Pages of the Sovereign** — Inside-the-book montage (map fragments, runes, silhouettes, tagline text) intercut with macro cover shots; percussive, faster pacing. Still-led animation.
4. **Whisper Teaser (15s, vertical)** — Ribbon flutter, gem glint, title and release line. Social cut reusing assets from concept 1 or 2.
